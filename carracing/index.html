Training an expert policy and distilling robust student policies using imitation learning and generative trajectory augmentation in CarRacing-v2.

Project Overview

Goal: improve continuous-control imitation learning robustness when student policies drift off the expert’s trajectory distribution.

Expert: PPO Student: CNN policy Data: expert rollouts + synthetic On-policy correction: DAgger

Methods

  • Train PPO expert and export rollout dataset (obs/action/terminal).
  • Train diffusion model on trajectories to generate synthetic supervision.
  • Behavior cloning distillation into a lightweight student network.
  • DAgger loop to label on-policy states with expert corrections.
